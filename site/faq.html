Frequently asked questions

About MindMender

What is MindMender?

A free website for looking after your mental health day to day. You can talk things through with an AI listener, take well-known self-checks for depression, anxiety and stress, log your mood, keep a journal, and use short breathing and grounding exercises.

Is MindMender therapy, or a replacement for a doctor?

No. MindMender can't diagnose, prescribe or treat anything. It's a place to notice how you're doing and find ways to cope. If you're struggling, please talk to a doctor, counsellor or someone you trust.

What should I do in a crisis?

Call a helpline now. In India: Tele-MANAS 14416 or KIRAN 1800-599-0019. In the US: call or text 988. Elsewhere: findahelpline.com. If you are in immediate danger, call 112 in India or your local emergency number. The "Get help" button at the bottom of every page shows these numbers too.

Does it cost anything?

No. Everything on MindMender today is free.

The AI chat

How does the chat work?

Replies come from a small language model we trained on publicly available question-and-answer conversations between people and counsellors. It's meant to listen and offer general coping ideas, not advice about your specific situation, and it can get things wrong.

Before a reply reaches you, a filter removes things the model shouldn't say, like links, phone numbers, names of doctors, or claims to be a therapist. If your message mentions suicide or self-harm, you get helpline numbers instead of an AI reply.

Are my chat messages saved?

No. Each message is sent to our server to produce a reply and is not stored by MindMender. Closing or clearing the chat removes it from your screen. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Self-checks

How accurate are the self-checks?

The depression (PHQ-9), anxiety (GAD-7), stress (PSS-10) and wellbeing (WHO-5) checks are standard questionnaires that doctors and researchers use for screening. A score is a signal worth paying attention to, not a diagnosis. Only a qualified professional can diagnose a condition.

Can I share my results with my doctor?

Yes. After a check, use "Print or save as PDF" to keep a copy you can show them.

Your data

Who can see my results and mood check-ins?

Only you. They're stored with your account, and the database's security rules only let a logged-in person read their own entries. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Who can read my journal?

Only you, when you're logged in. Entries are stored with your account under the same security rules as your check-ins, and we never use them to train the chat model. The people who run MindMender can technically open the database for maintenance; we only look at personal entries if you ask us to fix a problem or the law requires it. You can delete any entry from the journal page.

Can I delete my account and data?

A delete button inside the site is on the way. Until it's ready, see your choices in the Privacy Policy.

Do you use cookies or trackers?

We store a few things in your browser to keep you logged in and remember settings like dark mode. If you allow it, we also keep the campaign tags of the link you arrived from. There are no ads and no third-party tracking tools.

Using the site

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. MindMender works in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers.

How do I find something quickly?

Use the search button in the header, or press / on a keyboard.
