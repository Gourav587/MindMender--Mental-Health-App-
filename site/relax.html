Relax

Box breathing

Breathe in for 4, hold for 4, out for 4, hold for 4. Four rounds take about a minute.

Ready

5-4-3-2-1 grounding

When your thoughts are racing, bring yourself back to the present. Slowly notice:

  1. 5 things you can see
  2. 4 things you can touch
  3. 3 things you can hear
  4. 2 things you can smell
  5. 1 thing you can taste

Quick things that help

  • Drink a glass of water and stretch your neck and shoulders.
  • Step outside or look out a window for two minutes.
  • Write down what's worrying you, then one small next step.
  • Put your phone away for 15 minutes.
  • Text or call someone you trust.
  • Try to keep a regular sleep time tonight.
